Survival, local control and toxicity from landmark randomised trials. Personal reference, not a clinical decision tool.

Figures are author-reported point estimates from each trial; populations, eras, staging (FIGO 1988/2009) and endpoint definitions differ, so cross-trial comparison is indicative only. "Typical range" = span of arm-level estimates across the listed trials, not a meta-analysis.
